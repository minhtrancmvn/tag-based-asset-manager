# Privacy Policy

Tag-based Asset Manager. Effective 8 November 2026.

Tag-based Asset Manager is a desktop app that catalogs and tags files in folders you choose. It does not collect, send, share or sell personal data.

## What the app stores, all on your device

- App settings: the folders you add as libraries, your saved searches and your theme, kept in the app's own data folder on your computer.
- Tags and notes: kept in `.asset-tags.json` files inside the folders you tag.
- Backups: JSON files written only where you choose when you export.

## What the app does not do

- No account or sign-in.
- No analytics, telemetry, advertising or crash reporting.
- The Microsoft Store edition makes no network requests of its own; the Microsoft Store delivers its updates. The edition downloaded from GitHub checks GitHub for updates when it starts and when you ask; that request contains no data about you or your files, and GitHub receives standard connection details such as your IP address under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- Nothing is sent to the developer or to third parties.

## Access to your files

The app reads only the folders you add. It writes tag files in those folders, writes a backup file where you choose, copies a file path to the clipboard when you ask, opens files and folders in other apps when you ask, and moves items to the Recycle Bin or Trash only after you confirm. It never changes the contents of your files.

## Microsoft and Windows

The Microsoft Store and Windows components the app relies on, such as Microsoft Edge WebView2, handle downloading, installation and updates under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

## Removing your data

Removing a library in the app deletes its saved searches from the app's settings. Deleting the `.asset-tags.json` files in a folder removes its tags. You can uninstall the app at any time.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
